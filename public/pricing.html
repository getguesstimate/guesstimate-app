About the plans

What is a model?

A model is a spreadsheet containing your numbers, estimates, and calculations. You can try one in the scratchpad without an account.

Who can see my work?

Anyone can view a public model. An individual private model is visible only to its owner. An organization’s private models are shared with its members.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Plans are billed monthly. You can change or cancel your subscription through the payment portal in your account.

What happens to private models if I cancel?

Your private models become unavailable to view or edit until you subscribe again.

How can I pay?

Visa, Mastercard, and American Express are accepted.

Are there education or nonprofit discounts?

Yes. Contact us to ask about a discount for your organization.
