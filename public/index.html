Guesstimate

A spreadsheet for things that aren’t certain.

Open scratchpad Browse public models

No account needed to try. Scratchpad changes aren’t saved.

Example: project cost

You expect a project to take 40 to 60 hours, at $80 an hour. Guesstimate uses those numbers to work out a likely range for the total cost.

Working with estimates

Ranges

Enter a number when you know it. When you’re unsure, enter a range such as 40 to 60. You don’t have to settle on one number before you can start.

Formulas

Connect cells as you would in a spreadsheet. For example, multiply hours by an hourly rate to estimate a cost. Change either number and the result updates.

Results

See a range of possible answers and which are more likely, based on your estimates. The chart helps you see how much the result could vary.

Saving and sharing

Create an account to save your work and share it with others. Public models are free. Paid plans let you keep models private, on your own or with a team.
