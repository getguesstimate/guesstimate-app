Guesstimate

A spreadsheet for things that aren’t certain.

Open scratchpad Browse public models

No account needed to try. Scratchpad changes aren’t saved.

Example: project cost

A project takes 40 to 60 hours at a fixed rate of $80 per hour. Multiply the inputs to estimate the total cost.

Working with estimates

Ranges

Enter a value such as 80 or a range such as 40 to 60. Choose a distribution for each uncertain input.

Formulas

Refer to other cells in a formula, such as = A * B. Guesstimate carries their uncertainty through the calculation.

Results

Monte Carlo sampling produces a distribution of results. Inspect the mean, percentiles, and range for each cell.

Saving and sharing

Create an account in the app to save your own models. Public models are free; private models require a paid plan.
